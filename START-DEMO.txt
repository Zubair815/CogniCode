CogniCode Local Startup
=======================

Open two PowerShell terminals:

Terminal 1 - Backend (FastAPI + LangGraph):
  cd D:\CogniCode
  .\start-backend.ps1

Terminal 2 - Frontend (React + Vite):
  cd D:\CogniCode
  .\start-frontend.ps1

Open http://localhost:5173 in your browser.

Notes:
- The backend defaults to a local SQLite database at backend/cognicode.db and
  persists vector memory at backend/data/chroma.
- AI tutoring is configured via backend/.env (LLM_PROVIDER=groq and LLM_API_KEY).
- On /login, you can sign in with a registered Student or Instructor account,
  or use the one-click "Student demo" and "Instructor demo" buttons.
